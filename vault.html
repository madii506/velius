02 — One order, start to finish

What happens when you press Long.

  1. 01your browser

    You sign

    Your trading key signs the order on your screen. It can trade. It can never withdraw.

  2. 02the vault

    It checks

    Your margin, your leverage cap and the market's own limits, against the live book.

  3. 03Hyperliquid

    It mirrors

    The same order goes to Hyperliquid in the vault's name. The public book sees one more vault order.

  4. 04the vault

    You're filled

    At the price Hyperliquid filled the vault. Not a tick worse, not a spread on top.

  5. 05your book

    It's sealed

    The fill is written to your sealed book, encrypted to you. Only your screen opens it.

  6. 06the vault

    It watches

    Take profit, stop and liquidation, at Hyperliquid's mark, every hour of funding included.

03 — Rules

The rules it keeps.

The same rules Hyperliquid uses, so a position on Velius behaves like the one the vault holds for it.

MarginIsolated. Each position has its own margin, so a bad trade can only lose what you put behind it.
LeverageUp to 20x, and never above Hyperliquid's own maximum for that market. Where it allows 5x, so does Velius.
LiquidationAt Hyperliquid's mark price, once your margin falls to the maintenance level: half the initial margin at the market's maximum leverage.
Liquidation pricelong: entry × (1 − 1/L) ÷ (1 − m)
short: entry × (1 + 1/L) ÷ (1 + m)
m = 1 ÷ (2 × the market's maximum leverage)
FundingHyperliquid's hourly rate, paid or received on your position every hour, exactly as it lands on the vault.
FeesHyperliquid's fee is passed through at cost: 0.045% taker and 0.015% maker at the base tier. Velius's own fee will be published here before the vault opens.
OrdersMarket, limit and stop, with a take profit and a stop attached to any of them.
Minimum$10 an order, Hyperliquid's own minimum.
PriceEvery fill is at the price Hyperliquid fills the vault. The desk shows that book live.

05 — Solvency

Every position, mirrored.

The vault places every order it fills, so its position on Hyperliquid always equals the sum of the sealed books. Opposite trades net out on Hyperliquid, and the vault still settles each trader in full.

Its Hyperliquid account is public. When the vault opens, its equity and positions will be shown here, live, next to the total of the sealed books.

06 — Risks

Leverage cuts both ways.

Read these before you deposit anything. Only trade what you can afford to lose.

Custody

The vault holds funds

Like any exchange, the vault holds trading money while you trade. Withdrawals need your wallet's signature, but you are trusting the vault to honour them.

Venue

Hyperliquid's risks

The vault trades on Hyperliquid. Its outages, oracle prices and auto-deleveraging in extreme moves reach every position the vault holds.

Price

Gaps and liquidation

Prices can jump past a stop. At high leverage a small move reaches liquidation, and isolated margin on that position is lost.

Funding

It can turn

Funding is paid every hour, and the side that pays can flip. Holding against the crowd for days costs real money.

Software

New code

The vault engine is new and the Privacy Rings SDK is in alpha. Both are tested before opening, and neither is free of bugs.

Privacy

Not invisible

Deposits, withdrawals and the parties to a private transfer are public. The limits

08 — Questions

About the vault.

Why trade through a vault at all?

Hyperliquid's book is public, and so is every account on it. When the vault places your order in its own name, the book shows the vault's total, never your position.

Do I get a worse price?

No. You're filled at the price Hyperliquid fills the vault, plus Hyperliquid's own fee passed through at cost. Velius's own fee will be published here before the vault opens.

What if Velius goes offline?

Positions stay open on Hyperliquid in the vault's account, and stops keep working there. Withdrawals wait until it is back. That is part of the custody risk above.

Can the vault see my trades?

Yes. It fills them, so it has to. It keeps them off the public record: Hyperliquid's book shows only its total, and your sealed book is encrypted to you.

Can my trading key empty my account?

No. It can place and cancel orders. Moving money out takes your wallet's own signature.

When does it open?

When it is funded and has passed a live test on mainnet. This page and the desk switch to open on the same day, and not a day before.
