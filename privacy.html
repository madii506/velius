06 — Never

What Velius never does.

  • Ask for your seed phrase or private key
  • Keep your private wallet's keys; only its address is remembered, in this browser
  • Set cookies, run analytics or load trackers
  • Send your lines, sizes or stops anywhere; they are drawn on your screen
  • Give the trading key the power to withdraw
The only places this page talks to

The browser enforces it. This is the site's actual content policy for connections:

connect-src 'self' https://api.hyperliquid.xyz wss://api.hyperliquid.xyz

This site, for the chain reads it does on your behalf, and Hyperliquid's public API for prices. Nothing else can be reached from these pages.

07 — Limits

What privacy can't hide.

Stated plainly, so you can decide with all of it in view.

Public

Deposits show

Moving money into your private balance is a normal Solana transfer. Anyone can see it, and its amount, at that moment. Withdrawals back to a public wallet show the same way.

Public

Parties show

On the Default Ring, a private transfer's sender and recipient are public, with the owner's signature. Amount, asset and memo are hidden. Anonymous custom rings, which hide the parties too, aren't on mainnet yet.

Inference

Timing can be matched

A deposit followed at once by a large trade on Hyperliquid can be linked by time. Leaving time between them, and not moving round numbers, makes that harder.

Custody

The vault knows

It fills your trades, so it knows them. It keeps them off the public record; it can't keep them from itself. Only trade what you can afford to lose.

Market

Size still moves price

Hyperliquid's book shows the vault's total. A very large order still moves the market, and others can see that movement, if not whose it was.

Software

Early days

The Privacy Rings SDK is in alpha, and the vault isn't open yet. Both will change. This page will say so when they do.
