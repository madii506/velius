10 — Questions

Asked often.

What is Velius?

Private perp trading on Solana. You trade Hyperliquid's markets at Hyperliquid's price, and your positions sit in a sealed book that only you can read.

How is it private?

The vault places every order on Hyperliquid in its own name, so the public book shows the vault's total, never your trade. Money moves through Helius Privacy Rings, where amounts are encrypted.

What can people still see?

That your wallet uses Privacy Rings and sends to the vault. Not how much, and not what you trade. Your first move into a private balance is a normal, public deposit.

Who holds the money?

The vault holds trading funds, like any exchange does. Your trading key can trade but never withdraw, and withdrawals need your own wallet's signature. Only trade what you can afford to lose.

When can I trade?

When the vault is funded and has passed a live test on mainnet. The desk, the live markets and your private wallet work today.

Which markets, and how much leverage?

The perps listed on Hyperliquid, up to 20x and isolated. Where Hyperliquid allows less, so does Velius.

Does Velius see my data?

Market data streams from Hyperliquid's public API to your browser. Your lines and your private wallet are made in this tab. There are no cookies and no analytics. Once the vault opens, it knows your trades because it fills them; it never publishes them.

Is Velius part of Helius or Hyperliquid?

No. Velius is independent. It is built on Privacy Rings, the shielded pool Helius publishes as an open SDK, and reads Hyperliquid's public markets.
