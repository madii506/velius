How Velius works.

Everything from your first signature to the liquidation formula, with the numbers worked on Hyperliquid's live prices.

What Velius is

Velius is a private perp desk on Solana. You trade Hyperliquid's perpetual futures at Hyperliquid's price. The vault places each order on Hyperliquid in its own name, so the public book never shows yours, and money moves through Helius Privacy Rings, where amounts are encrypted.

Getting started

  1. Connect a Solana wallet. Phantom, Solflare or Backpack, from the button at the top right. Velius never asks for a seed phrase, and no page has a field for one.
  2. Derive your private wallet. Sign one message on the privacy page or in the desk's Private wallet tab. The Privacy Rings SDK makes the wallet in your browser, and the same signature always gives the same wallet back.
  3. Deposit. Move USDC into your private balance, then send it to the vault as a private transfer, its amount encrypted. Opens with the vault.
  4. Trade. Pick a market, set the size, leverage, take profit and stop, and place the order. It fills at Hyperliquid's price.
  5. Withdraw. Your wallet signs. The vault settles and sends a private transfer back to you.

The desk

One screen for one trader. Everything on it streams live from Hyperliquid's public API; your own lines never leave it.

Market bar
The market, its mark and oracle prices, the 24 hour change, volume, open interest, funding an hour and a year, and the countdown to the next payment. The star adds it to your watchlist.
Watchlist
Your starred markets with live prices, under the market bar. Click one to switch. It's kept in this browser only.
Chart
Candles from one minute to one day, with volume and the live mark. Moving averages over 20, 50 and 200 candles switch on above it. Your entry, take profit and stop are drawn as lines you can drag; the liquidation price is drawn too.
Book, trades, depth
Hyperliquid's live order book, grouped to the tick you pick; the public tape; and the book as two cumulative curves around the mid. Click a level to price a limit order there.
Ticket
Side, order type, size in dollars or by risk, leverage, take profit and stop. Entry, size, margin, liquidation, fee, the loss at your stop and reward to risk update as you type, and it warns you when liquidation comes before your stop.
Sealed book
Your positions, orders and history, encrypted to you, plus the market's funding over seven days and your private wallet.

Order types

Market
Fills now against the book. The ticket estimates your average entry by walking the live book for your size, and pays the taker fee.
Limit
Rests at your price and fills only there or better. A resting order pays the maker fee.
Stop
Triggers when the mark crosses your price, then fills as a market order.
Take profit and stop
Attach to any order. Type them, or drag their lines on the chart.
Size by risk
Switch the size field to Risk and type the most you would lose at your stop. The ticket works out the size, fees in and out included.

Funding

Perps never expire, so funding keeps their price close to the spot price. Every hour, one side pays the other.

  • Paid every hour, on the hour. The desk and the markets page count down to the next one.
  • A positive rate means longs pay shorts. A negative rate means shorts pay longs.
  • The rate is built from the premium, how far Hyperliquid's price sits from the oracle, plus a fixed interest part of 0.00125% an hour, which is 0.01% every eight hours.
  • Hyperliquid caps the rate at 4% an hour.
  • "A year" on Velius is the current hourly rate times 8,760. It's a pace, not a promise: rates change every hour.

Privacy

Your private wallet
Derived from one wallet signature over a fixed message, with the Privacy Rings SDK, in your browser. Its keys are never stored; the same signature recreates them.
The registry
Links your wallet to its private wallet, so others can send to you privately. Being on it is public.
The Default Ring
Amount, asset and memo are encrypted. The sender, the recipient and the owner's signature on each private spend are public, and so are deposits into the Ring and withdrawals out of it.
Coming later
Anonymous custom rings, which hide the parties too, aren't on mainnet yet.

The full model and the live Ring

The vault

The vault fills your order at Hyperliquid's price, places the same order on Hyperliquid in its own name, and records the fill in your sealed book, encrypted to you. Its position on Hyperliquid always equals the sum of the sealed books. It opens once it is funded and has passed a live test on mainnet.

How it works, its rules and keys

Security

No seed phrases
Velius never asks for one, and no page has a field for one. Wallets connect through the Wallet Standard; your wallet shows you everything you sign.
Keys with one job
Your wallet signs withdrawals. Your trading key places and cancels orders and can never withdraw. The vault's agent on Hyperliquid trades and can't withdraw; its main key is kept offline.
Locked connections
Pages can only connect to this site and Hyperliquid's public API: connect-src 'self' https://api.hyperliquid.xyz wss://api.hyperliquid.xyz. Scripts load only from this site, and the pages can't be framed.
Nothing tracked
No cookies, no analytics, no trackers. Your watchlist, timeframe and chart settings live in this browser only.
Server routes
They read public chain data and keep nothing. Each one is listed under data sources.

Glossary

Perp
A perpetual future: a contract that tracks a price, with leverage and no expiry.
Mark price
Hyperliquid's fair price for a market, built from its own book and outside prices. Liquidations and unrealized profit use it.
Oracle price
The spot price from major exchanges, updated by Hyperliquid's validators every few seconds. Funding is measured against it.
Mid price
Halfway between the best bid and the best ask.
Premium
How far the perp trades above or below the oracle.
Funding rate
What one side pays the other each hour, as a share of the position.
Open interest
The total size of every open position in a market.
Size
A position's value at the entry price: coins times price. Also called notional.
Leverage
Size divided by the margin behind it.
Isolated margin
Margin that belongs to one position only.
Maintenance margin
The least margin a position may keep before it's liquidated: half the initial margin at the market's maximum leverage.
Liquidation
The forced close of a position whose margin has run out.
Auto-deleveraging
Hyperliquid's last resort in extreme moves: closing profitable positions against ones that can't be liquidated.
Maker and taker
A maker's order rests on the book; a taker's fills against it. Makers pay less.
Spread
The gap between the best ask and the best bid.
Slippage
How far your average fill lands from the price you saw, because your size walked the book.
Tick
The smallest price step. Grouping the book merges levels into bigger ticks.
Reward to risk
What your take profit pays, divided by what your stop costs, fees included.
EMA
Exponential moving average: an average of closing prices that weighs recent candles more.
Privacy Rings
Helius's shielded pool on Solana, where balances and transfers are encrypted.
Default Ring
The live Privacy Rings pool: amounts hidden, parties public.
Private wallet
Your address inside Privacy Rings, derived from one signature.
Sealed book
Your positions and history at Velius, encrypted to you.
Agent
A Hyperliquid API wallet: it can trade for an account and can't withdraw from it.

Questions

Which markets can I trade?

Every perp listed on Hyperliquid, up to 20x and isolated. Where Hyperliquid allows less, so does Velius.

Is Velius part of Helius or Hyperliquid?

No. Velius is independent. It's built on Privacy Rings, which Helius publishes as an open SDK, and reads Hyperliquid's public markets.

Does Velius see my data?

Market data streams from Hyperliquid to your browser. Your lines and your private wallet are made in this tab. Once the vault opens, it knows your trades because it fills them; it never publishes them.

Why is the yearly funding so large?

It's the current hourly rate times 8,760. Rates change every hour, so treat it as a pace.

Where's my watchlist stored?

In this browser only. Clear the site's data and it's gone; nothing about it reaches Velius.
